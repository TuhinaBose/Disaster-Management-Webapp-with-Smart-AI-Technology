Disaster Safety Guidelines & Preparedness

Official survival instructions, emergency checklists, and action protocols approved by disaster management authorities.

BEFORE a Flood
  • Know your area's flood risk and evacuation routes.
  • Keep emergency kit ready with 3 days of clean drinking water.
  • Move valuable electronics and important documents to upper floors.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances if water enters premises.
DURING a Flood
  • Move immediately to high ground or upper levels of sturdy buildings.
  • NEVER attempt to walk or drive through moving flood water.
  • Avoid touching electrical equipment or fallen power lines.
  • If stranded on roof, signal for rescue using a bright cloth or flashlight.
AFTER a Flood
  • Listen to official news before returning home.
  • Boil all drinking water to prevent waterborne diseases.
  • Do not enter damaged buildings until inspected.
  • Report damaged utility lines to authorities immediately.
BEFORE an Earthquake
  • Secure heavy furniture, mirrors, and bookshelves to walls.
  • Identify safe spots in each room (under sturdy tables).
  • Conduct regular earthquake drills with family members.
DURING an Earthquake
  • DROP, COVER, and HOLD ON!
  • If indoors, stay inside. Cover head and neck under a sturdy table.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings, streetlights, and utility wires.
  • If driving, pull over safely away from overpasses and stop.
AFTER an Earthquake
  • Expect aftershocks. Be prepared to drop and cover again.
  • Check for gas leaks and electrical damage. Shut off main valves if suspected.
  • Wear sturdy shoes to protect feet from broken glass.
BEFORE a Cyclone
  • Secure loose roof tiles, doors, and window shutters.
  • Trim dead tree branches near your house.
  • Store emergency food, water, battery bank, and torchlight.
DURING a Cyclone
  • Stay indoors in the strongest part of the building.
  • Keep away from windows and glass doors.
  • Do not be fooled by the calm "eye" of the cyclone; winds will resume suddenly.
AFTER a Cyclone
  • Remain inside until officially declared safe.
  • Watch out for fallen trees, electrical wires, and sharp debris.
  • Drink boiled water to avoid contamination.
BEFORE a Fire
  • Install smoke alarms on every level of your home.
  • Keep fire extinguishers accessible and learn how to use them (PASS).
  • Plan and practice two escape routes out of every room.
DURING a Fire
  • GET OUT, STAY OUT! Never go back inside.
  • Crawl low under smoke to avoid toxic gas inhalation.
  • If clothes catch fire: STOP, DROP, and ROLL.
AFTER a Fire
  • Seek medical attention for burns or smoke inhalation.
  • Do not re-enter premises until fire department gives clearance.
  • Notify insurance provider and disaster authorities.
BEFORE a Heatwave
  • Stock oral rehydration salts (ORS), lemon water, and fluids.
  • Cover windows with curtains or reflective shades.
  • Check on elderly neighbors and vulnerable individuals.
DURING a Heatwave
  • Avoid outdoor exposure between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
  • Drink plenty of water even if you do not feel thirsty.
  • Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored cotton clothes.
  • Never leave children or pets inside parked vehicles.
HEATSTROKE FIRST AID
  • Move person to a cool, shaded place.
  • Wipe body with cold damp cloth or apply ice packs to armpits/neck.
  • Call emergency helpline 108 immediately.
