Life Safety

72-Hour Survival Preparedness

Be ready before disaster strikes. Keep an essential emergency kit ready for yourself and your family.

1. Water & Nutrition

Store 3-4 liters of water per person per day, plus 3 days of non-perishable food (granola bars, canned goods, dried fruit).

Hydration Priority
2. First-Aid Supplies

Pack antiseptic, sterile bandages, burn ointments, adhesive tape, scissors, ORS sachets, and 7-day personal prescription medications.

Medical First
3. Power & Comms

Battery-powered LED flashlight, charged 20,000mAh power bank, spare AA/AAA batteries, emergency whistle, and portable radio.
